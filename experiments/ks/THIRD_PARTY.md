# KS simulator attribution

The simulator in `prepare_data.py` is adapted from the KS stepper in the author's
`Entropy/Entropy-1/KS/KS_test.ipynb`. The companion source `KS_Simulate.py`
attributes the original spectral stepping implementation to:

- Felix Köhler, *Machine Learning and Simulation*,
  https://github.com/Ceyron/machine-learning-and-simulation
- Copyright (c) 2021 Felix Köhler
- MIT License

The observation extraction, initial perturbation, noisy training split and CLI
are adaptations for these experiments.
